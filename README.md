# VarimiTrust

**Growing together, rooted in the land.**

VarimiTrust is an independent, volunteer-led farming community in Zimbabwe. Farmers, families, and friends share knowledge and practical support to strengthen rural livelihoods and care for the land.

[Visit varimitrust.com](https://varimitrust.com) · [Get in touch](https://varimitrust.com/#contact) · [Join the community](https://varimitrust.com/#join)

## What we do

VarimiTrust brings members together to support one another through:

- **Peer learning** — sharing practical advice on soil, water, storage, and marketing.
- **Community activities** — organizing field days, training, and seasonal gatherings.
- **Shared resources** — coordinating group orders for seeds and supplies where available.
- **Collective projects** — giving members a voice in shared initiatives and community funds.
- **Farm-focused technology** — exploring field data, mapping, records, and agritech pilots.

## How we work

- **Community-led:** Decisions stay with the community; VarimiTrust is independent and is not a government agency.
- **Trust and cooperation:** Members share tools, knowledge, and encouragement across seasons.
- **Fair and respectful:** The community values honest advice, safe shared spaces, privacy, and fair dealing.
- **Practical and inclusive:** Farmers, farm workers, agriculture students, and neighbours are welcome.

## Areas of interest

The website highlights opportunities and activities across:

- **Agronomy:** soil and water stewardship, livestock, and pasture management.
- **Technology:** farm records, field data and GIS, agritech pilots, and equipment support.
- **Operations and markets:** production, finance, market analysis, and connections between farmers and buyers.
- **Communications:** sharing field stories and useful information through clear, accessible channels.
